# Version %VERSION% (Build %BUILD%)

### Status Panel

- Added a Bold Panel Text switch that also applies to the selected input and output device names (#364).
- Volume, brightness, and microphone sliders support automatic, preset, and custom colors. Changes apply immediately, and the fill reaches the right edge at 100% when you release the slider (#359).
- The scrollbar follows the macOS Show scroll bars setting. The Bluetooth heading tint and audio device spacing were also refined (#360).

### Icons & Rings

- The custom ring's charging indicator follows Battery > Top marker. Fixed missing bolt and plug icons in the left/right layout, and made the same settings available on the Battery and App Icon pages (#361, #362).

### System Monitor

- The SSD capacity ring has a customizable color, and the network, fan, and disk detail pages follow their metric colors.
- Corrected the App Memory and Memory Used calculations to match Activity Monitor's definitions (#363).
- Added CPU and GPU temperature readings for the M5 and M6 families and expanded Intel CPU core temperature coverage (#358).
- GPU and network top-app lists show their first readings sooner, and reopening a detail page shows the previous ranking while it refreshes (#365).
